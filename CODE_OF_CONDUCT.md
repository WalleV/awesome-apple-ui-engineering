# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

## Expected Behavior

- Discuss resources and engineering trade-offs with specific evidence.
- Give and receive editorial feedback in good faith.
- Disclose relevant project, employer, or commercial relationships.
- Respect differing technical choices and platform constraints.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the guide and its maintenance.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or undisclosed promotional activity.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction on scope or conduct.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or restrict participation when behavior conflicts with these expectations.

Report sensitive concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/WalleV). Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. If no private contact method is available, open a minimal issue requesting a private reporting channel without describing the incident publicly.

Reports will be considered as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
